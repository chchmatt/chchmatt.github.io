How high can you climb?

HomePrivacySupport

You're an escaped prisoner at the bottom of an endless tower — and the police are on your tail. There are no stairs, and every lift is out of order… so draw your own.

  • Swipe up or down to doodle an elevator, and ride it.
  • Draw an arch for a door to hide behind.
  • Draw a line for a wall to stop the police in their tracks — for a while.
  • Draw a box for a window — the police stop to look out of it.
  • Tap a lamp to plunge a floor into darkness.

The higher you go, the more police there are, and the wiser they get. How high can you climb before they catch you?
