How do I play?

Tap TUTORIAL on the home screen: it walks you through drawing elevators, doors, walls and windows, and breaking lamps, step by step.

My drawing turned into graffiti!

That happens when a stroke isn’t recognised as anything — or there’s no room for it. Elevators are straight swipes up or down across at least two floors; doors are an arch on one floor; walls are a short straight line down; windows are a box on the wall.

Sound, music and vibration

Tap the gear on the home screen: there are switches for sound effects, music (with a volume slider) and vibration.

The phone’s buttons keep popping up

A swipe that starts right at the bottom edge of the screen can bring them up. In Settings, turn on Show navigation buttons to keep them showing.

Everything’s too small

In Settings, set View size to Comfortable: 7 floors on screen instead of 9, so everything is bigger.

Too hard?

In Settings, set Difficulty to Relaxed: slower police who don’t see as far, and quieter floors. It keeps its own high score.

Reset my high score

Settings → RESET HIGH SCORE.
